## Definition

Trade-balance closure specifies the relationship between national income, expenditure, and net external transfers. A counterfactual may hold deficits fixed, scale them, or eliminate them subject to global adding up.

## Economic Intuition

When expenditure exceeds income, the economy absorbs more than it produces. Removing that transfer requires relative wages, production, sourcing, and absorption to adjust until world trade is mutually consistent.

## Literature Lineage

Dekle, Eaton, and Kortum (2008) use observed deficits in the baseline and calculate counterfactual equilibria in which current-account imbalances are eliminated.
