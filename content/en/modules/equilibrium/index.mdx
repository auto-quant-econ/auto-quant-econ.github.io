## Economic Role

The equilibrium module closes the model. It specifies which markets clear, which budgets balance, who receives profits and rents, how external deficits are treated, and which nominal variable provides the numeraire.

## Baseline Specification

The baseline jointly clears goods and labor markets, imposes free entry, rebates local housing rents to residents, and balances trade. Wages, price indices, trade shares, population, and rents adjust until all conditions hold.

## Choosing a Specification

Choose market structure according to the source of markups and variety, equilibrium scope according to the policy question, land ownership according to incidence, and deficit closure according to whether international saving and borrowing matter.

## Literature Map

Dekle, Eaton, and Kortum (2008) shows how observed trade imbalances can enter exact-change counterfactuals. Spatial models add rent ownership, labor mobility, and local market-clearing conditions that materially affect welfare incidence.
