## Economic Role

The idea-flows module determines whether productivity is fixed or responds to knowledge created elsewhere. It separates static spatial reallocation from mechanisms that change the technology frontier itself.

## Baseline Specification

The baseline treats location productivity as an exogenous fundamental. This isolates trade, migration, and market-clearing mechanisms and provides a disciplined benchmark for assessing richer spillovers.

## Choosing a Specification

Introduce localized spillovers for innovation clusters, diffusion for convergence, endogenous innovation for research policy, adoption for catch-up, and imperfect transferability for multinational or cross-border knowledge questions.

## Literature Map

The literature ranges from reduced-form measures of knowledge diffusion to quantitative growth and spatial models in which local research, market access, and the transferability of ideas jointly determine regional productivity.
