## Economic Role

The production module maps factor prices and productivity into marginal costs, output, factor demand, firm entry, and product variety. Together with trade costs, it determines a location's delivered competitiveness in every destination.

## Baseline Specification

The baseline firm uses labor, faces a fixed labor requirement, and produces at constant marginal cost. CES demand and monopolistic competition imply a constant markup; free entry links firm mass to local employment.

## Choosing a Specification

Select input–output linkages for supply-chain propagation, multiple factors for distributional incidence, firm heterogeneity for selection and extensive margins, and endogenous productivity when agglomeration is part of the research mechanism.

## Literature Map

Canonical spatial and trade models differ sharply in production structure: monopolistic-competition models emphasize entry and variety, whereas Eaton–Kortum formulations emphasize comparative advantage and probabilistic sourcing.
