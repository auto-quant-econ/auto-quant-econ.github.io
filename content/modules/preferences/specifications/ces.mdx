## Definition

A CES aggregator converts quantities of differentiated goods into a composite consumption index. The elasticity parameter governs substitution across origins or varieties.

## Core Equations

CES expenditure minimization delivers origin expenditure shares and an exact price index. In gravity models, changes in those shares summarize how sourcing responds to changes in relative delivered costs.

## Economic Intuition

A higher substitution elasticity makes expenditure more responsive to relative prices. When origins become cheaper, consumers shift sourcing toward them more strongly.

## Literature Lineage

CES demand is a central building block in quantitative trade and spatial economics, including exact-change counterfactuals based on observed bilateral expenditure shares.
