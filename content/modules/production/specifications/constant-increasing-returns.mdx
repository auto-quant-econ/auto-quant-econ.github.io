## Definition

Under constant returns, doubling all inputs doubles output and competitive pricing equates price with unit cost. Increasing-returns formulations introduce fixed costs, markups, entry, or external scale economies.

## Economic Intuition

The returns-to-scale choice determines whether spatial concentration changes productivity or variety, and whether firms have meaningful scale and entry decisions.

## Literature Lineage

Dekle, Eaton, and Kortum (2008) use constant returns and perfect competition. Monopolistic-competition spatial models instead use fixed costs and free entry to generate endogenous varieties.
