## Economic Role

Migration reallocates workers and population across space. The module connects nominal wages, consumption prices, housing costs, amenities, and moving frictions to location choices, local labor supply, and welfare.

## Baseline Specification

The baseline is a static discrete-choice model. Workers compare indirect utility across destinations; idiosyncratic preference shocks prevent everyone from selecting the same location. Housing congestion makes local costs respond to population.

## Choosing a Specification

Use static destination choice for long-run population allocation. Add bilateral costs for origin–destination flows, dynamics for adjustment paths, residence–workplace choice for commuting, worker types for distributional effects, and congestion when travel costs respond to network use.

## Literature Map

Allen and Arkolakis (2014) combines trade and mobility in a spatial equilibrium. Monte, Redding, and Rossi-Hansberg (2018) separates residence from workplace choice. Caliendo, Dvorkin, and Parro (2019) uses dynamic labor reallocation to study transition paths.
