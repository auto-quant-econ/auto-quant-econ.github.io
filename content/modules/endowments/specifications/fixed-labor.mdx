## Definition

The aggregate labor endowment of each country is fixed. Depending on the counterfactual horizon, workers may move between domestic sectors or remain attached to their baseline sector.

## Economic Intuition

Sectoral mobility absorbs shocks by reallocating labor inside a country. When labor is sector-specific, relative sectoral wages carry more of the adjustment burden.

## Literature Lineage

Dekle, Eaton, and Kortum (2008) compare mobile- and immobile-factor cases to distinguish long-, medium-, and short-run adjustment.
