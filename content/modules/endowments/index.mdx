## Economic Role

The endowments module records the local resources that production, consumption, and mobility act upon. Whether an endowment is fixed, mobile, or accumulated changes both incidence and the horizon of a counterfactual.

## Baseline Specification

The baseline fixes total population and each location's housing stock. Labor is homogeneous and capital is absent. Population can move, but scarce housing generates a congestion force through rents.

## Choosing a Specification

Add skills for distributional analysis, elastic housing for zoning and city growth, mobile capital for investment responses, accumulation for dynamic horizons, and infrastructure when policy directly changes spatial connectivity.

## Literature Map

Urban and spatial models differ in their treatment of land and housing supply; trade and development models add capital, skills, and infrastructure to measure heterogeneous incidence and adjustment.
